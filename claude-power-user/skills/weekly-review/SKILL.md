# Weekly review

1. **Look back:** read this week's `daily/` and `meetings/` notes, sent email and completed Todoist tasks. Write 5 bullets on what moved and what didn't.
2. **Dropped balls:** find promises I made in email or meetings with no follow-through, emails with no reply after 3+ days, and open actions older than 7 days.
3. **File the inbox:** move each item in `inbox/` into the right PARA folder, or turn it into a task.
4. **Priorities:** propose a rewritten `current-priorities.md` (top 3, waiting on, not doing) and ask me to confirm.
5. **Improve the system:** list every correction I gave Claude this week (from daily-note logs and git history). Propose concrete edits to context files or skills. Suggest one new skill or routine based on repeated work.
6. Write the result to `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` under `## Weekly review` and commit.
