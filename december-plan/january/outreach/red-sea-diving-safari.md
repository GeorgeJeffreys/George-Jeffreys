Dear Reservations team,

I am organising a scuba trip for the Wharton Outdoors Club, the MBA outdoor club at the University of Pennsylvania, and Marsa Shagra is our preferred base. I am a former PADI instructor and will be travelling as a diving guest. Could you quote the following for arrival Sunday 3 January 2027 (evening) and departure Saturday 9 January 2027, six nights, full board, twin sharing?

Group scenarios: 15, 20 and 25 people. In each case about half are complete beginners who will finish PADI Open Water eLearning at home before travel, the rest are certified divers, and roughly 15% of the group want Advanced Open Water or Elphinstone-level diving.

Please send:
1. The winter 26-27 euro price list (PDF) for Marsa Shagra and confirmation that 3–9 January is priced as low season. Per person per night in a Royal Tent and a Deluxe Chalet, and whether any night falls in a "feast period" with non-refundable terms.
2. PADI Open Water course price with equipment, and whether the €94 e-code is waived if students buy PADI eLearning directly. How many instructors and certified assistants you can commit to 8–13 Open Water students Monday 4 – Wednesday 6 January, and the ratio you run in the bay.
3. The 5-day house-reef package, the extra-day price, Advanced Open Water, Enriched Air Nitrox (course and fills), scuba review for anyone not diving in the last 12 months, and daily equipment rental for certified divers, including 5 mm suits and hoods.
4. Excursions: Dolphin House, Abu Dabab and speedboat dives with their fees; Elphinstone by day boat and by speedboat; seat capacity of each boat and zodiac so we can see how a group of 25 is split; minimum certification for Elphinstone.
5. Group terms: confirmation that the 11th and 21st (or 22nd) guest receive free room, transfers and dive package, exactly what the free place excludes, and whether one free place can be applied to the organiser.
6. Transfers per person one way from Marsa Alam and from Hurghada airports at 10–20 and 21–30 people, and the visa plus meet-and-assist fee.
7. Deposit amount and date, balance date, the cancellation ladder that will apply to our dates, whether individual participants can drop out and at what cost, and whether we can pay by bank transfer or card, in one payment or per participant.
8. Weather: what happens to pre-paid excursions cancelled by wind (refund or credit), and confirmation that the house-reef package is unaffected.
9. Safety: the chamber-support fee, the day boats' licensing and safety equipment, and any incidents in the last five years.
10. Availability for 13 twin rooms on those dates and how long you can hold them.

We would like to confirm within a few weeks of receiving the quote. Thank you very much.
