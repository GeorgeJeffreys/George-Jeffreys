Dear Emperor Divers team,

I am organising a land-based scuba trip for the Wharton Outdoors Club, the MBA outdoor club at the University of Pennsylvania, for 3–9 January 2027 and would like a day-diving and course quote for Port Ghalib, with the same for Sharm el-Sheikh as a comparison. I am a former PADI instructor travelling as a guest. No liveaboards, please.

Group scenarios: 15, 20 and 25 people; about half are complete beginners who will finish PADI Open Water eLearning at home, the rest certified, about 15% wanting Advanced Open Water and Elphinstone.

Please quote, per person:
1. PADI Open Water for eLearning-complete students over Monday 4 – Wednesday 6 January: price with equipment, whether it is priced without the digital materials, the pool you use for confined water, and how many instructors and certified assistants you can commit to 8–13 students at once.
2. Day diving Monday 4 – Friday 8 January for 8–12 certified divers (Friday morning only): the 2-dive day-boat price on your "no hidden extras" basis, the third-dive price, equipment rental including 5 mm suits and hoods, and any package price for 4 or 5 days.
3. Advanced Open Water and Enriched Air Nitrox supplements; scuba review for anyone not diving in 12 months.
4. Elphinstone by speedboat (single dive) and by day boat (two tanks): prices, minimum certification and seat limits.
5. Your boats: how many day boats operate from Port Ghalib in January, seats per boat, and whether a group of 20–25 gets its own boat.
6. Group terms: the group-free-place ratio for day diving and courses, whether it can be applied to the organiser, and what it excludes.
7. Deposit and cancellation for a group of this size (your terms mention a 20% non-refundable deposit for groups of six or more and a 100% charge within 30 days; please confirm what would apply to us and to an individual who drops out), and whether participants can pay individually by card.
8. Weather: how you handle days the port captain closes the harbour or wind cancels offshore sites – refund, credit or alternative sites – and typical January cancellation rates at Port Ghalib.
9. Safety: boat licensing and safety equipment, your incident record over the last five years, and the chamber you use.
10. Hotel partners at Port Ghalib within walking distance of the jetty, if you have group rates.

For Sharm, the same items plus Ras Mohammed, Tiran and Thistlegorm supplements and their January cancellation experience.
