# Egypt programme, Sat 2 – Sat 9 Jan 2027 (six nights on the ground, Sun 3 – Sat 9): the rounded trip plan

Basis: one dive village doing lodging, food, diving and transfers under one contract (primary Red Sea Diving Safari at Marsa Shagra; fallback Blue Ocean with Abu Dabbab Lodge, which has now replied), participants fly themselves to Hurghada or Marsa Alam. Safety: OW 18 m, AOW 30 m, Deep 40 m; last dive out of the water by 12:00 Fri 8; no liveaboards; Elphinstone and Dolphin House encounters are natural, unbaited. Every evening at 18:30 there is a 15-minute briefing (template in `shared/briefing_template.md`). All euro prices are the brief's leads and QUOTE NEEDED until RSDS or Blue Ocean replies.

## Groups

- Beginners (about half): PADI Open Water, eLearning done at home (buy the e-code through the centre if cheaper), confined water in the sheltered bay, four OW dives on the house reef, Mon–Wed.
- Certified: unlimited house-reef diving (north and south drops by zodiac), two day-boat excursions, optional speedboat outer-reef dives.
- Advanced (roughly 15%): AOW across the week, Elphinstone day boat, night dive, nitrox where offered.

## Off afternoon (Thu 7, from about 13:30)

- Desert afternoon from the village: quad bikes or a 4x4 to the Wadi el Gemal edge, Bedouin tea, sunset and stargazing back at the village (RSDS and Blue Ocean both offer desert excursions; price QUOTE NEEDED, ADVERTISED elsewhere around EUR 30–50).
- Wadi el Gemal national park half-day (mangroves, the Hamata coast) for the nature-minded; longer drive, so only if the group wants it.
- Port Ghalib marina evening as the alternative for a town fix: cafes, shisha, the marina walk; taxi about 45 minutes from Marsa Shagra.
- Not recommended: a Luxor day trip is four to five hours each way and would swallow a dive day.

## Contingency

Wind: the house reef dives from the bay in almost any wind; day boats are the first casualty, so Elphinstone and Dolphin House get a second attempt on Thu. Illness or a missed connection: the village runs orientation dives daily, so a late arrival slots in a day behind.
