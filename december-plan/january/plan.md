## Conclusions

1. Base: **Marsa Alam, at Red Sea Diving Safari's Marsa Shagra village** (confidence: medium-high, ~70%). It is the only candidate where beginners train and everyone fun-dives from the shore on a sheltered house reef, so north wind does not stop the core programme; it bundles full board, lodging, diving and transfers in one contract with published group free places; it has a recompression chamber on the premises; it is outside Sinai; and January is its low season. Cost at Marsa Shagra, 6 nights Royal Tent full board plus diving, transfers and fees, is about USD 1,000–1,150 per person before flights (INFERRED from unverified brief leads), so the USD 2,700 ceiling holds if the PHL–Europe–Egypt fare stays under about USD 1,500. 3. The 7-night window gives Mon 4 – Fri 8 on the ground: three OW course days (Mon–Wed), two fun-dive days for the new divers, five dive days for the certified, with Fri 8 a morning-only dive day so everyone is 18 h clear of a Sat 9 flight.

## Capacity analysis

- PADI ratios (snippets of PADI standards): confined water 10 students per instructor plus 4 per certified assistant; open-water training dives 8 per instructor plus 2 per certified assistant to a maximum of 12. So 8 beginners need one instructor plus an assistant on the open-water dives; 13 beginners need two instructors (or one instructor and two certified assistants for 12, with the 13th in a second group). The outreach email asks each operator for the number of instructors and assistants they will commit Mon–Wed. George's own instructor status is a fallback only if he is in teaching status and insured; the plan does not rely on it.
- Certified 8–12: RSDS's day-1 orientation dives are guided, so two guides are needed on Mon 4; thereafter house-reef diving is self-guided in buddy pairs and does not consume guides. Zodiac and speedboat drops cap at their seat count (QUOTE NEEDED) and are the constraint for outer-reef days, not guides.
- Excursions: Dolphin House and Elphinstone day boats carry a full group, so a 25-person trip will need two boats or two days; the email asks for boat seat counts.
- Lodging: 131 units at Marsa Shagra (aggregator), so 25 people in 13 rooms is not a capacity problem in low season, but 1–10 Jan attracts European holidaymakers; availability QUOTE NEEDED.
- Fallback split: if Shagra can take the certified divers but not all beginners, Emperor Port Ghalib (pool, 10 min from RMF) or Blue Ocean Abu Dabbab (10 km) can run a second OW cohort with the group reuniting for Dolphin House and Elphinstone days; this loses the single-contract simplicity.

## Weather contingency

- North wind is the January hazard. It cancels Elphinstone (exposed offshore pinnacle), Dolphin House less often, and makes speedboat drops uncomfortable. The house reef and Abu Dabab bay are sheltered and stay diveable, so the OW course, the certified group's daily dives and the AOW core dives are all wind-proof; only the advanced subgroup's Elphinstone day is at risk, and Wed/Thu give it two attempts.
- Fallbacks in order: house-reef north and south walls by zodiac; Abu Dabab; Marsa Egla and the inner-range sites in the "excursion package" (structure ADVERTISED (snippet)); a second Dolphin House day.
- Money: ask whether cancelled boat excursions are refunded or credited, and whether the 5-day house-reef package is unaffected (it should be, being shore-based).
- Water 21–24 °C: 5 mm suits with hoods; ask whether rental includes 5 mm and hoods in January.
- Sharm/Hurghada contingency, if either is chosen instead, is weaker: wind closes Tiran and Ras Mohammed and the operators fall back to Naama Bay local reefs or Giftun lee sites; there is no shore programme for beginners beyond the pool.

## Risks

- Prices unverified: every euro figure is a brief lead; the winter 26-27 page is blocked. Mitigation: the outreach email asks for the PDF.
- Feast-period terms: 1–10 Jan may carry non-refundable deposits despite low season (RSDS policy ADVERTISED (snippet)). Ask for group-specific terms and a named cancellation ladder.
- Group-threshold discrepancy: brief says 11th/21st/30th guest free; FAQ snippet says 11/22/33. Only the 30-person case differs; ask.
- Instructor capacity for 8–13 OW students in three days; and whether George's teaching status changes the free-place count.
- Flights: RMF has few scheduled European links; an HRG arrival adds a 3-h coach each way and an 18-h fly-after-dive check on Sat 9 is unaffected but the Fri afternoon becomes travel-free anyway.
- Boat safety: Sea Story capsized off Marsa Alam in Nov 2024 (18 dead including missing declared dead); Hurricane fire near Elphinstone June 2023 (3 dead); Sindbad submarine Hurghada Mar 2025 (6 dead); a Hurghada day-boat fire and a Gulf of Suez liveaboard sinking in July 2026 (DIVE Magazine snippet). All were overnight boats or a submarine except the July 2026 day boat. The plan keeps the group on shore except for two optional day-boat days; the email asks for boat certification, life-jacket and radio provisions, and the operator's incident record.
- Insurance: Egypt is an "impacted country" for the Middle East known-event exclusion (W5). Refundable operator terms matter more than usual.
- Sinai optics avoided at Marsa Alam; Level 2 advisory still applies nationwide.
- Cold: 21–24 °C water; students in 5 mm suits with hoods; shorten sessions if anyone chills.
