Dear MBA Student Life team,

The Wharton Outdoors Club is planning three member-funded scuba diving treks for 2026-27, following the club's Aruba scuba trek in December 2024. Before we take any deposits or sign with vendors we would like to run the plans past you and confirm the rules that apply to MBA club travel, since we could not find them published on MBA Inside. The trips are:

1. Thanksgiving, 10 certified divers, La Parguera, Puerto Rico (US territory). Preferred dates Wed 25 – Sun 29 Nov 2026; an alternative of Thu 19 – Tue 24 Nov exists but falls on class days. A variant would go to Grand Bahama for day-boat shark dives (these are baited dives and would be labelled as such).
2. December, 20–40 people, about half beginners taking the PADI Open Water course, Cozumel, Mexico, Tue 8 – Tue 15 Dec 2026 (or Tue 8 – Sun 13). Core exams end Mon 7 Dec; the Penn final-exam period runs Thu 10 – Thu 17 Dec, which affects some second-years.
3. January, 15–25 people, about half beginners, Marsa Alam or Hurghada, Egypt, Sat 2 – Sat 9 Jan 2027, land-based only (no liveaboards), travelling via Europe. Egypt is US State Department Level 2 overall, with Level 4 zones in North and Middle Sinai and parts of the Western Desert that are far from our base.

Participants are MBA students, many international, and possibly partners; no undergraduates. I am a former PADI instructor but would take part purely as a participant; all instruction would be by the local operator.

Our questions:

1. Approval: what is the approval or notification process for a club-organised trek, who signs off, and what lead time do you need for each of the three dates above? Is there a form (the undergraduate division uses a Club Trip Notification Form) and a trip-leader training we should complete?
2. Academic days: are club treks permitted on class days, and does the Penn final-exam period (10–17 Dec) affect approval of the Cozumel dates? If class days are excluded we will drop the 19–24 Nov option.
3. Partners: may partners join a club trek, and if so do they sign the same waiver and are they treated as guests of the club for insurance purposes?
4. International students: is there any guidance we should pass on (for example, Penn ISSS advice on travel documents for Puerto Rico, or anything specific to F-1/J-1 students going to Mexico, the Bahamas and Egypt)?
5. Egypt: given the Level 2 advisory with regional Level 4 zones, and the current Middle East insurance "known event" exclusions, is Egypt acceptable as a club trek destination, and would you want Penn Global or CITRA to review it even though graduate students are exempt from the petition requirement?
6. Insurance and waivers: is there a required Acknowledgement of Risks / Hold Harmless template for MBA club travel, and any minimum insurance you require? We intend to make DAN membership plus a DAN dive-accident plan mandatory for every diver and to recommend trip-cancellation cover.
7. Money: must all participant payments go through CampusGroups, or may the operator invoice participants directly for the balance with only the deposit collected through the club? Are there WGA rules on deposit size, refund policy wording, or holding participant funds?
8. Organiser's place: some operators offer one free place per group. Should this be disclosed to participants, treated as a club asset (spread as a discount), or is there a rule that the organiser may not benefit personally?
9. Advisor: must a faculty or staff advisor be informed or involved, and does the trip count as Penn-affiliated travel (which would require MyTrips registration and give International SOS cover) if it is entirely participant-funded?
10. Emergency protocol: do you require an emergency plan, a roster with next-of-kin, and daily check-ins, and who at Wharton should be the emergency contact?
11. Alcohol: what is the alcohol policy for club trips off campus?
12. Vendor contracts: may we contract with dive operators and hotels in the club's name, who is authorised to sign, and are there terms you require (for example, refundable deposits or force-majeure wording)?

We are happy to come in and walk through the plans. A short reply on questions 1 and 2 would let us fix the dates and open sign-ups; the rest can follow.
