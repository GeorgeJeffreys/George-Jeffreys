# Evening briefing template (every evening, 18:30, 15 minutes, hotel common area)

Run by George. Same shape every night so people know what to expect; keep it to a quarter of an hour and finish with questions.

1. Today in one minute: what happened, anyone certified, any incidents or near-misses (no names), kit issues.
2. Tomorrow by group: beginners, certified, advanced. For each: meeting point and time, pickup, boat or shore, sites, expected depth and time, who leads.
3. Rules of the day: depth limits (OW 18 m, AOW 30 m, Deep 40 m), buddy pairs, surface interval, hydration, no alcohol before diving (Sand Dollar and Blue Ocean both refuse hungover divers), sun and seasickness.
4. Weather and port call: what the operator has said; what changes if the port closes (shore plan) or a boat is late.
5. Money and admin: anything to pay the operator tomorrow, forms still missing, eLearning records, park fee cash.
6. Off-programme options: night dive, afternoon dive, the off-afternoon activity, dinner plan.
7. No-fly reminder on the last dive day: last dive out of the water by the time in the programme; nothing after.
8. Questions.

Post the same content in the trip WhatsApp group straight after, with the times in bold.
