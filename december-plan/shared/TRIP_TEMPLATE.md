# Trip template — how every club dive trip is built (from the Cozumel work, 23 Sep 2026)

Use this for Thanksgiving and Egypt, and for any future trip. File names in brackets are the Cozumel originals to copy.

## 1. Shape
- Dates fixed around the academic calendar (core exams, finals, George's diary). One dive centre for all divers; one hotel; everyone shares rooms; participants book their own flights.
- Tracks: Beginner (PADI Open Water, eLearning at home, three days in-water), Certified (two-tank every morning, a third dive offered most afternoons), Advanced (AOW, nitrox, deep sites, night dive) inside Certified.
- Rhythm: arrival day with paperwork and gear fitting; course days; certification by the middle of the week; one off afternoon we organise ourselves (an island loop, a boat to a sandbar, the desert); group boat days when everyone is certified; last dive at least 18 hours before flights; farewell evening.
- Every evening 18:30, a 15-minute briefing (shared/briefing_template.md), then the social side.
- Programme document per trip [cozumel/PROGRAMME.md]: groups, day-by-day table by track, off afternoon, add-ons, contingency, money and forms.

## 2. Outreach and choosing the centre
- First-contact email [cozumel/outreach/final/*.md]: casual, brief, George as former PADI MSDT leading, group shape, dates, what we want to know (capacity for the whole group, prices at three sizes, leader place, deposit and refund ladder, hold-until date). Then chase after two business days, reserve wave the same day.
- Save every reply [cozumel/replies/], put quoted numbers into the workbook, answer facts yourself, escalate commitments.
- Compare on a per-head basis for a certified diver and a student, diving plus lodging plus meals, comps and commission counted [cozumel/OPERATOR_COMPARISON.md]; add the qualitative read (location, boats, ratios, reputation, how they were over email) [cozumel/WEEK_BOARD.html].

## 3. Pricing rule and budget
- One trip fee covers everything diving-related (course or package, all gear rental, park fees, eLearning) and the hotel with breakfast, two sharing. DAN or equivalent dive-accident cover is required but bought by each person (George, 24 Sep). Own costs: flights, meals, drinks, tips, airport transfers. Budgets are shown to participants without flights.
- Inside the fee: George's flight allowance, half-room and rental; an odd-headcount room risk; a tax buffer only where a quote is known to be pre-tax (Cozumel: zero, George 24 Sep); a 6% contingency refunded if unused. Fee set at a conservative pricing headcount (20) and rounded up to the nearest 100; check the surplus stays positive at the minimum headcount (12) and grows with size.
- Commission from the operator goes to George; spare comps reduce the club's invoice; all disclosed on the sign-up page.
- Deposits: USD 400 at sign-up, balance about five weeks out; deposits must cover the operator's and hotel's deposits at every size; refund ladder mirrors the operator's.
- Builder [pricing/build_cozumel_budget.py]: copy, change the Inputs block, run, recalc with LibreOffice. Sheets: Inputs, Fee, Group (12/20/30/40), Payments.

## 4. Advertising
- Flyer image generated by George from a facts-only prompt [cozumel/FLYER_PROMPT.md]: dates, place, tracks day by day, off afternoon, hotel and centre, leader, fees and what they cover, guidance budget without flights, add-ons, sign-up steps and deadlines, tone. No design direction.
- Check the generated flyer against the programme: beginners dive on the group boat days too; certified dive mornings with optional afternoons; last-dive timing on the right day; "after finals" only if true for the whole group.

- Before departure: deputies optional (George keeps facilitation), waivers and medical forms, DAN in each name, eLearning codes, park-fee cash float, WhatsApp group with the briefing posted nightly.
