1. **Puerto Rico, Wed 25 – Sun 29 Nov, fits the $1,500 ceiling**: core all-in band **$1,010–1,640 pp at 10** (midpoint ~$1,325) for flights, four nights, three dive days (six dives, gear included on the Wall days), cars and the bio bay, before meals. 2. **Tiger Beach does not fit**: two BAITED shark days at $399 + VAT (2021 price; VAT now 10%, so ~$439/day), Old Bahama Bay at $205–600 a room and PHL–FLL–FPO at ~$520–810 give **~$1,840–2,360 pp at 10** (midpoint ~$2,100), i.e. **~$775 pp more than Puerto Rico** (range ~$700–850), under sales-final terms that convert a weather cancellation into a one-year credit. Opt-in variant only.
3. **19–24 Nov is the wrong window**: it costs four Wharton class days (Thu 19, Fri 20, Mon 23, Tue 24), it is the window the **Colombia trek** has historically occupied (about 17–23 Nov, so trekkers are home for Thanksgiving; INFERRED from the 2024 edition), and it has the worst bio-bay moon of the month. The preferred 25–29 window clears the trek, misses at most one class day (Wed 25 Nov, probably a class day on the 2025-26 pattern) and gives a dark bio-bay window on Sat 28 Nov.
4. **The Baleària ferry is a real alternative for 25–29 Nov only**: its typical Wed/Sun sailings match those dates (verify), but the 09:00 departure forces a Tuesday night in Fort Lauderdale, which erases most of the saving against Bahamasair's $271–313 return.
5. **Top gaps**: (a) 2026 Thanksgiving-week PHL–SJU fares and an AA/Frontier group hold; (b) Paradise Scuba's boat capacity for 10–12 on one departure, group rate, leader place and Thanksgiving-Day operation; (c) Neal Watson's 2026 per-day price and VAT line (the $399 + 12% snippet is from 2021).

Safety rules applied throughout: OW 18 m, AOW 30 m, Deep 40 m; no dive within 18 h of a flight; no liveaboards; shark dives labelled BAITED.

## 1. Puerto Rico plan (La Parguera base)

### 1.1 Why La Parguera
The Parguera Wall is a ~20-mile drop-off on the sheltered south-west (lee) coast, 20–45 min by boat from the village, with sites such as Black Wall, Fallen Rock, Hole in the Wall, Old Buoy, The Chimney and Efra's Wall at 18–37 m. Wall profiles sit at the AOW limit; OW-only divers must be paired on the shallower sites and held to 18 m. November is normally calm on the south coast (INFERRED); hurricane season officially ends 30 Nov. La Parguera has the only Puerto Rico bio bay in which swimming is allowed, though it is the dimmest of the island's three, so the moon matters (1.6).

### 1.7 Desecheo weather odds
Desecheo lies ~13 nm west of Rincón and is exposed to the North Atlantic winter swell that arrives from November. Taino advertise daily departures "weather permitting" with local off-shore dives as the fallback (ADVERTISED, snippet). INFERRED cancellation probability for a late-November day: 30–50%. Treat Desecheo as an upgrade decided 48 h out; a third Wall day with Paradise ($125, gear included) is the default and costs $15 less.

## 2. Tiger Beach variant (Grand Bahama) — BAITED shark diving

### 2.1 Product
Tiger Beach is a 5–8 m sand bank on the Little Bahama Bank north-west of West End; Neal Watson describe **50-plus-mile runs across the Gulf Stream with unpredictable weather windows** (ADVERTISED, snippet). **All dives are BAITED** (crated bait; divers kneel in a line; safety divers with poles). Water in late November is PRIOR KNOWLEDGE ~24–26 °C, falling to the low-70s °F by January; a full 5 mm suit, hood and dark gloves are needed for long static bottom times. Minimum certification is Open Water; comfort/experience criteria QUOTE NEEDED.

Bahamas VAT is **10% since 1 Jan 2023** (was 12% from July 2018) — https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/service/value-added-tax-vat ; PwC tax summaries, 21 Sep 2026 — so the brief's 12% is stale; model **$439/day** and treat the operator's VAT line as QUOTE NEEDED. ## 5. Risks
1. **Fares**: nine weeks out in Thanksgiving week; Frontier's $62–70 base is an early-November figure, not a holiday one. Hold an AA or Frontier group block by early October.
2. **Thanksgiving Day operations**: Thu 26 is a holiday in Puerto Rico; Neal Watson advertise daily trips but minimum numbers apply. Confirm boats run.
3. **Lodging demand**: Thanksgiving weekend is a Puerto Rico domestic peak (INFERRED); the village has ~150 rooms across four hotels.
4. **Hurricane season** runs to 30 Nov; a late-season system cancels everything. Trip insurance with a weather clause (W6); Neal Watson themselves recommend DAN trip-cancellation cover.
5. **Desecheo**: 30–50% cancellation (INFERRED); default to a Wall day ($15 cheaper, gear included).
6. **Tiger Beach terms**: sales final, one-year credit only; a blown-out day leaves a ~$439 credit that graduating students may never use; 50-mile open-water runs.
7. **Depth discipline**: Wall sites tempt beyond 30 m; brief on AOW/OW limits and pair by certification.
8. **Cars**: three vehicles or van plus car; under-25 surcharges; toll tags.
9. **Bio bay**: dimmest of the three PR bays; book Sat 28 and set expectations.
10. **Dive-accident insurance**: DAN 2026 plans Master from $44/yr, Preferred from $79/yr, Guardian $126.50/yr plus DAN membership (price not in snippet) — ADVERTISED (snippet, https://dan.org/safety-prevention/diver-safety/divers-blog/membership-dive-accident-insurance-comparison/, 21 Sep 2026).
