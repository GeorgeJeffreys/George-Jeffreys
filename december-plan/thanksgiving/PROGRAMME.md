# Thanksgiving programme, Wed 25 – Sun 29 Nov 2026, La Parguera, Puerto Rico: the rounded trip plan (23 Sep)

Basis: 8–12 certified divers, one boat with Paradise Scuba & Snorkeling Center (12 on one vessel, quoted 22 Sep), Parador Villa Parguera (quote pending), three hire cars from San Juan inside the group budget, participants fly PHL–SJU themselves. Safety: last dive Sat by noon for Sunday flights; no baited shark dives; Desecheo optional and only if a boat with capacity turns up. Evening briefing 18:30 each night (shared/briefing_template.md). Off-afternoon activities are ours to plan.

## Add-ons (opt-in, at cost)
- No third dive the same afternoon: Paradise dives air only and advises against it on nitrogen loading (QUOTED 24 Sep); the night dive on Fri is the extra dive instead (USD 140 list, about 158 after discount, tax and gear). Sunset Bio Bay Cruise Sat (USD 85 list, about 81 after discount and tax; eat, cruise, swim). Itinerary agreed with Paradise 24 Sep; Paradise opens on Thanksgiving morning for us.
- Desecheo day trip only if Island Style Charters (4 divers per charter) or another boat can take the group in two runs; treated as optional.

## Contingency
Wind on the south coast rarely stops the Wall boat; if it does, Paradise reschedules or refunds (to confirm). Hurricane season formally ends 30 Nov; a named storm means the club's own refund ladder applies and travel insurance is each person's own.
