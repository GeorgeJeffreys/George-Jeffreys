🐢🌊 *COZUMEL LOGISTICS* 🌊🐢
Sun 13 – Sat 19 Dec · Blue Note Scuba · Hotel Plaza Cozumel

*1. 📋 Fill in your row on the trip sheet (by Fri 20 Nov)*
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Dwswoxle77W-eMUIqrXBaDjOxPJ9jLn4sC0HNWFNohM/edit?usp=sharing
One sheet for everything: your email and date of birth (for the PADI eLearning), gear sizes, flights, roommate and any dietary needs. Find your name, fill in your own row only, and come back to update it as you book flights and tick things off.

*2. ✈️ Getting there: Sun 13 Dec*
• Fly into *Cozumel (CZM)* and land by *4pm*, or
• Fly into *Cancún (CUN)* and land by *1pm*. Then take the ADO bus to Playa del Carmen (about 1h15, ado.com.mx) and the Ultramar ferry to Cozumel (about 40 min, ultramarferry.com). Hotel Plaza is a short walk from the ferry pier.
• Sunday evening: Doug from Blue Note meets us at the hotel to go through the week and put the beginners into groups of 4 with their instructors. Time to follow.

*3. 🏨 Hotel*
Hotel Plaza Cozumel, two to a room, breakfast included. Check in Sun 13, check out Sat 19.

*4. 🗓️ The week*
• *Mon 14*: Beginners do confined water at Tikila Beach. Certified divers go out on the boat (taxi to the marina).
• *Tue 15*: Free morning. Beginners do Open Water dives 1–2 in the afternoon. Certified divers take the afternoon boat.
• *Wed 16*: Beginners do Open Water dives 3–4 from the boat in the morning. Certified divers take the morning boat. Afternoon off.
• *Thu 17*: Free morning. Everyone takes the afternoon boat together. Optional night dive.
• *Fri 18*: Everyone takes the morning boat. Last dive is out of the water by 1pm. Group dinner in the evening.
• *Sat 19*: Fly home. Any Saturday flight is fine for flying after diving.

*5. 🛟 DAN dive insurance: everyone, by Fri 27 Nov*
Buy it through Blue Note's link: https://apps.dan.org/scuba-dive-insurance/?rc=w1695003
That's about $84 for membership plus the Master plan. Mark it done in the sheet.

*6. 📚 Beginners: PADI eLearning*
Make sure your email and date of birth are in the trip sheet. I'll order everyone's codes in one go through Blue Note. Your medical form and waiver come with the eLearning.
If you answer YES to anything on the medical form, you need a doctor's sign-off *before* the trip.
Finish the eLearning by *Sun 6 Dec*. It takes 8–12 hours, so start early.

*7. 🪪 Certified divers*
Send me a photo of your cert card (front and back) and the date of your last dive by *Fri 27 Nov*. Blue Note will send your forms with the boat confirmations.

*8. 🤿 Gear*
All gear is included. Add your height, weight and shoe size in the trip sheet. Certified divers: say if you want a dive computer ($10 a day). Beginners get one free during the course.
If you have a mask that fits you well, bring it.

*9. 💸 Payments*
If you haven't finished paying, please send the rest by *Fri 13 Nov* ($1,400 beginners / $1,250 certified, minus any deposit you've paid). Pay by Wise with your name + "Cozumel" in the reference.

*10. 🎒 Pack*
Passport (valid for the whole trip) · cert card (certified divers) · reef-safe sunscreen · swimsuit + rash guard · light jacket for the boat · cash for tips, meals and drinks · seasickness tablets if you get queasy

Questions? Message me any time 🙌
