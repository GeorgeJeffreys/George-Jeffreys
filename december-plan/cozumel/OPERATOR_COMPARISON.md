## Reading it
- Blue Note is the cheapest for certified divers by a wide margin once the 10 per cent commission and one-in-five comps are counted, its AOW and nitrox courses are free with eLearning, its deposit is 25 per cent with refunds to 7 days out, and its contracted Hotel Plaza rate is the cheapest bed on the list. Open question: how many instructors it puts on 10–20 students, and whether park fees are extra.
- Sand Dollar is the strongest on the beginner side (16 per cohort, second cohort at 1 pm, shore training site, DAN cover in the price, 28-diver boat, 6:1) and sits next to Casa del Mar. Its boat days for our week are Mon am, Tue am, Wed pm, Thu am, Fri pm: the Fri pm boat collides with Saturday flights, so the week would end with Thu as the last boat day unless they can swap Fri to the morning (asked).
- Aldora is the premium boat operation (steel 120s, 4:1 on courses, five instructors for 20 students) and all-in on tax and park fees, but the dearest, and it will not pencil anything without USD 100 per person.
- Dive Paradise is the only one-roof option: 180-room resort, all meals and drinks in, dedicated boat at 16+, comps on rooms as well as diving, 10 per cent commission. It is the dearest for certified divers before comps and commission, close to Sand Dollar after them, and its lodging rate expires on 30 Sep unless Lisa can extend it (asked). Course prices still to come.
