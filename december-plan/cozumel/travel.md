## Conclusions

1. Approach Hotel Cozumel & Resort first (180 rooms, breakfast included, Dive Paradise on site, 1.6 km from downtown): the only property that can hold 40 in one block, at an advertised c. USD 41–54 per person per night at 2 sharing (INFERRED c. 30–40 at 3 sharing). Casa Mexicana with sister hotels Suites Bahia and Suites Colonial is the downtown alternative at c. USD 65–84 pp (Casa Mexicana) or USD 38–50 pp (Suites Bahia), breakfast included at both.
2. Fly PHL–CZM on American via Charlotte for both versions: advertised return band USD 568–800 (brief band USD 432–803), one stop, 7–9 h door to door. The Cancún nonstop plus ADO bus and Ultramar ferry saves c. USD 150–350 pp (c. USD 315–450 all-in) but takes 9–11 h and adds two weather-exposed links; use it as the fallback for price-sensitive members on the 7-night version only.
3. Lodging fits the ceilings comfortably: 7 nights at 2 sharing is USD 290–590 pp at every recommended property, leaving USD 1,400–1,700 for flights, diving and fees against the USD 2,000 / 2,300 ceilings.
4. Group air: American's Group & Meeting Travel desk (10+ passengers, deposit per head, holding fee of the three lowest deposits, one free name change to 48 h) is the natural fit; Frontier and Spirit group desks cover only PHL–CUN nonstops.
5. Biggest gaps: no hotel publishes group, deposit or cancellation terms; no Scuba Club Cozumel price at all; and no airline fare for the exact dates. All three are written into the outreach drafts.

Why this order. Capacity drives the December choice more than price: 40 people at 2 sharing is 20 rooms, at 3 sharing 14, and half the group needs a pool and a shore site for confined-water sessions on Wed–Fri. Hotel Cozumel & Resort has both and an operator with big boats on its own pier; it is a 20-minute walk or 5-minute taxi from the downtown bars. Casa Mexicana is the better address and the higher-rated hotel, and the three sister properties together could plausibly take 30–40 people, but at USD 65–84 pp it costs roughly USD 100–200 more per person over 7 nights than the resort and has no pool suited to training. The Blue Magic package is the cheapest certified-diver bundle on the island (USD 884 for lodging plus 10 boat dives, before the USD 15 daily park fee and gear) but Hotel Plaza is small and beginners need a separate course quote. Casa del Mar is the cheapest resort with a published triple policy. Scuba Club Cozumel is on the list because it keeps diving from shore when the port closes, which matters on a December trip, but it published nothing readable. Villablanca (USD 26–45 pp, breakfast extra), Hotel Barracuda (c. USD 35 pp, own pier) and Hotel Flamingo (USD 27–44 pp) are cheaper overflow options; Blue Magic Hostel gives free dorm nights to its dive customers and three free nights to Open Water students, which is the cheapest beginner bed on the island though not "decent hotel" standard; Cozumel Palace (USD 109–277 pp per night all-inclusive) breaks the ceiling and is listed for reference only.

Distance to the shortlisted operators (INFERRED from published addresses and km markers). Aldora Divers' shop and pier are at Calle 5 Sur 37, 100 m from Casa Mexicana and walkable from every downtown hotel; Aldora also picks up at south hotel-zone piers. Salty Endeavors' shop is at Puerto Abrigo in the north but its boats collect divers at a chain of downtown and hotel-zone piers (Aldora pier, Blue Angel, Fonatur and others). Blue Magic's shop and Hotel Plaza are in the centre. Dive Paradise runs from the Hotel Cozumel pier (km 1.7) and Scuba Club Cozumel from its own dock (km 1.5). Sand Dollar Sports is at km 3.2, next to Villablanca. Blue Note Scuba and Scuba Tony both depart from Marina Fonatur (Blue Note's address is Costera Sur km 6, ASIPONA Fonatur marina), about 10 minutes by taxi from downtown; Scuba Tony picks up at hotel piers south of the marina only and offers no land transport, so a downtown hotel means a daily taxi to the marina for those two operators. The practical rule: downtown hotels suit Aldora, Salty Endeavors and Blue Magic; the resort strip (km 1.5–4) suits Dive Paradise, Scuba Club, Sand Dollar and pier pick-ups; Marina Fonatur operators need a taxi from either.

### Recommendation

Both versions: PHL–CZM on American with one stop, preferably Charlotte. American is the only carrier on CLT–CZM (about 10 nonstops a week, departures 09:05–09:49, 3 h 14 m; FlightConnections snippet), so the outbound is an early PHL departure and an early-afternoon Cozumel arrival on Tue 8 Dec; Miami and Dallas connections exist on AA and Houston on United. Aggregator bands: Expedia lists PHL–CZM returns from USD 568, Momondo a typical range of USD 607–2,026, Cheapflights quotes AA at USD 608 as the cheapest carrier; the brief's USD 432–803 band sits inside this. Treat USD 568–800 as the planning band for both the 8–15 Dec and 8–13 Dec versions (ADVERTISED (snippet), QUOTE NEEDED for the exact dates). Door to door 7–9 h each way (INFERRED). Every snippet confirms there is no nonstop on Tuesdays or Sundays.

Timing on the short version: with a Sun 13 morning departure (any flight after about 08:00) the last dive must finish by about 14:00 on Sat 12, which allows the standard two-tank morning boat but not an afternoon dive. On the 7-night version the Sun 13 dives leave 40+ h before a Tue 15 flight; the Mon 14 spare day should stay dry if it is used for diving after a norther only in the morning.

The Saturday AA nonstop: the route ran seasonally on Saturdays in winter 2022–23 (10:40 departure, 4 h 40 m, Travel Off Path) and FlightsFrom shows the last operated flight as 10 Dec 2022; no snippet confirms a 2026–27 season. Even if it runs, it fits neither version (QUOTE NEEDED; reference row in `flights.csv`).

### Cancún plus ferry alternative

PHL–CUN nonstop (American, Frontier and Volaris per Skyscanner, 25 nonstops a week, 4 h 09 m; Spirit was named in the brief but did not appear in any snippet, so verify) is advertised from USD 255–379 return on Kayak, USD 180 on Expedia, and Frontier showed a one-way fare of USD 196 on Mon 7 Dec. Add the ADO airport bus to Playa del Carmen at MXN 208–270 (c. USD 13–16) one way, 1 h 15 m including stops at Terminals 3, 4 and 2, with departures roughly every 20 minutes through the afternoon; the ADO terminal at Juárez and 5th Avenue is three blocks (5–15 minutes with bags) from the ferry pier. Ultramar's Premium Plus fare is MXN 290 (c. USD 16) one way, USD 31 return, with a port fee of about MXN 65 paid at a separate booth; Winjet charges MXN 285; both sail every 30–60 minutes from about 06:00 to 23:00 from Playa and about 05:00 to 22:00 from Cozumel, crossing 35–45 minutes. All-in: c. USD 315–450 pp return (ADVERTISED (snippet), QUOTE NEEDED for the dates), 9–11 h door to door each way (INFERRED: flight 4 h 09 m, immigration and bags 1 h, bus 1 h 15 m, walk 15 min, ferry wait up to 30 min, crossing 45 min, hotel walk or taxi). For a group, private 15-seat vans from the airport to the ferry pier are advertised from USD 95 per vehicle (Cancun Shuttles), so three vans for 40 people cost about the same per head as ADO and remove the terminal-hopping and sell-out risk (ADO warns that airport routes sell out daily). Frontier's group desk (10+ on nonstop routes only, one fare for all, seat and bag included, USD 5 pp on acceptance then USD 40 pp within five business days when booked 60+ days out, names due 21 days before, USD 50 name change inside 21 days) and Spirit's (10+, USD 40 pp non-refundable deposit if 60+ days out, free name changes to 30 days before, USD 65 change fee at 31–59 days, no changes inside 30 days) apply here.

Recommendation: offer the Cancún routing only on the 7-night version, and only as an individual option, because a Sunday-morning norther can suspend the ferry and strand people with a same-day Cancún departure.

## Risks

- Northers. Nortes run October–March and peak December–February; the ScubaBoard tally for 2010–17 counted 9 December port closures over the eight years, most lasting hours to a day and occasionally two (ScubaBoard, Sand Dollar Sports, Salty Endeavors pages). Both ferry lines suspend sailings when the port closes, so the Cancún routing on the short version carries a real chance of a missed Sunday flight; the 7-night version's Mon 14 spare day absorbs one closure for the diving but not for a Sun 13 ferry. Mitigation: fly PHL–CZM, or on the Cancún routing take the first ferry with an afternoon CUN departure and buy refundable or changeable fares; hotels with shore diving (Scuba Club, Hotel Cozumel, Villablanca) keep the beginners' training on schedule.
- Flying after diving. The Sun 13 return needs the last dive to end by about 14:00 on Sat 12 for a flight after 08:00 Sunday; publish this rule in the participant terms.
- High season pricing. Blue Magic's package steps from USD 884 to USD 1,143 on 21 Dec, and Dive Paradise's USD 635 package is valid only to 20 Dec, so 8–15 Dec sits just inside the shoulder band; do not let dates slip later.
- Capacity and blocks. No hotel publishes block terms; Casa Mexicana's 88 rooms and Hotel Plaza's small size make 40 people in one downtown hotel unlikely in December, so a split block (Casa Mexicana plus sisters, or Hotel Cozumel for all) needs a decision early.
- Room taxes and fees. Cozumel hotels add environmental and resort fees (Casa del Mar USD 9 per room per night; Hotel Cozumel c. USD 2) and 16 percent VAT plus a lodging tax on top of some advertised rates; ask every hotel for a tax-inclusive per-room figure.
- Unverified airline group deposits. AA's per-passenger deposit amount and United's terms were not readable; get them in writing before collecting the club's USD 575-style deposit.
- Web access. Nothing above was read on a primary page; the outreach emails ask each hotel to confirm every number.
