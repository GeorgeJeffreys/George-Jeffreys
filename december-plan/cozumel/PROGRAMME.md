# Cozumel programme, Sun 13 – Sat 19 Dec 2026: the rounded trip plan (revised 24 Sep: alternating boat times)

Basis: one dive centre for everyone, Blue Note Scuba (George's choice, 23 Sep; shop at Marina Fonatur, km 6, short taxi from town), one hotel, Hotel Plaza in downtown San Miguel (contracted through Blue Note), participants fly themselves in. Safety: OW 18 m, AOW 30 m, Deep 40 m; last dive out of the water by 13:00 Fri 18; no dive after that. Every evening at 18:30 there is a 15-minute briefing for the next day (template in `shared/briefing_template.md`). Off-afternoon and add-on activities are planned by us, not by the shop.

Shape (George, 24 Sep): everyone dives two tanks a day, but the certified boat ALTERNATES between morning and afternoon through the week so that two mornings are free (Tue after the first night out, Thu after the certification dinner). Optional extra dives sit in the other half of the day and are priced at cost, so anyone can stop at two dives a day. CONFIRMED by Doug 24 Sep: the boats can alternate morning and afternoon (he adds that the afternoon is the better slot for the new divers, when the reefs are quieter, and that it gives weather slack); two boats (14 + 10) are blocked for the week.

## Groups

- Beginners (about half): PADI Open Water with Blue Note, eLearning finished at home before travel, three days Mon–Wed (Mon confined water at Tikila beach, Tue OW dives 1–2 at the training site, Wed dives 3–4 from the boat), then two boat days with everyone on Thu and Fri. USD 400 course incl. equipment plus USD 240 for the two boat days (QUOTED). Maximum 4 students per instructor (QUOTED).
- Certified: five two-tank boat days Mon–Fri on the 5-day package (USD 600 less 10% commission to George; park fee USD 13 a boat day cash on the island), timed Mon am, Tue pm, Wed am, Thu pm, Fri am. Optional extra dives: Mon afternoon two-tank boat (about USD 145 with park fee), Tue and Thu morning shore dives at Tikila Beach Bar (USD 60 incl. gear, QUOTED; coral nursery and webcam site), night dive Thu evening. Most will do the two boat dives a day and stop.
- Advanced (roughly 15%): AOW free with Blue Note when the AOW eLearning is done at home (about USD 220), nitrox course free with eLearning (fills about USD 12), the deeper walls with the certified group once signed off.

## Off afternoon (Wed 16, from about 13:30)

Pick one for the whole group, or split into two:
- Island loop by rented jeeps and scooters: San Gervasio Maya ruins, Punta Sur eco park (lighthouse, crocodile lagoon, Celarain beach), the east-side beach clubs (Chen Río or Punta Morena) for a late lunch. Best all-round option; not weather-dependent. ADVERTISED: jeep rental around USD 60–90 a day, park entries about USD 10–20 each (QUOTE NEEDED).
- Catamaran or lancha to El Cielo and Colombia lagoon for the sandbar, starfish and rays: good for anyone not diving that afternoon and for partners. Shallow snorkelling only, so it does not affect the no-fly or repetitive-dive picture. ADVERTISED: about USD 50–80 pp (QUOTE NEEDED).
- Town afternoon: chocolate workshop (Kaokao), tequila tasting, the seafront and the market; the lowest-cost fallback if the port is closed or it rains.

Nothing on Wed afternoon is at depth for divers who have three more dive days ahead, and the day before the group boat days stays alcohol-light.

## Contingency

Port closure on a training day: Sand Dollar's shore site keeps confined water and the shallow OW dives going in the lee; a closure on Wed pushes OW dive 4 to Thu morning and the new divers join the boats Fri. Two closed days early in the week mean a PADI referral to finish at home. Certified divers on a closed day: shore dives at Coral Gardens and the off-afternoon activity pulled forward.

## Sidemount for George (researched 23 Sep, nobody asked)

Cozumel is almost entirely single-tank recreational, but sidemount is doable for personal practice:
- Aldora is the shop that explicitly accommodates sidemount divers on its boats (ScubaBoard, ADVERTISED snippet), with 100–120 cf steel tanks; heavy for sidemount but workable.
- Jungle Divers rents 60 and 80 cf pro-valve tanks (the left/right valves sidemount wants) and teaches PADI Sidemount; Cozumel Dive School (PADI CDC) runs the PADI Sidemount and Tec Sidemount courses; Bluedream Cozumel lists sidemount under SSI/PADI/TDI.
- With whichever centre the group uses, the practical ask at booking time (not before) is two AL80s per dive with standard valves, an extra-tank fee, and boat space for the rig; bring your own harness, wing and two regulators (one long hose, one on a necklace) since no shop lists sidemount rental. On a 28-diver boat or Blue Note's smaller boats it is a courtesy call for the divemaster rather than a problem. Sources: scubaboard.com sidemount Cozumel thread; jungle-divers.com rental page; cozumeldiveschool.mx specialties; bluedreamcozumel.com.

## Where to stay: the three bases (active MBA crowd)

- Downtown San Miguel (Hotel Plaza via Blue Note at USD 70–88 a room with breakfast; Casa Mexicana): the bars, restaurants, the seafront and the ferry are on foot; Blue Note and Aldora are minutes away; group dinners and late nights need no taxis. Downside: no beach or house reef, and if the centre is Sand Dollar every student needs a taxi to km 3.2 each morning (about USD 8–10 a cab, 10 minutes).
- South hotel zone km 3–4 (Casa del Mar, being priced; Villablanca): 200 yards from Sand Dollar's shore site, pool and beach-club access, quieter nights; taxis or a group shuttle into town for dinner (10 minutes). Best if Sand Dollar is the centre, and the 8:30 walk to the shop matters with 20 students.
- Cozumel Hotel & Resort all-inclusive at km 1.7 (Dive Paradise's base): everyone under one roof with pool, pier, meals and domestic drinks included, which keeps a 30–40 person group together and takes the food logistics away; 20 minutes' walk or a 5-minute taxi to town, so nights out still happen. Downside: resort bubble, the 30 Sep rate deadline, and diving tied to Dive Paradise.
