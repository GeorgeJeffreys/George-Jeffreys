Off afternoon, add-ons, options

Wed 16, from 13:30: the off afternoon (we organise it)

  • Island loop by rented jeeps and scooters: San Gervasio Maya ruins, Punta Sur eco park (lighthouse, crocodile lagoon, Celarain beach), late lunch at an east-side beach club (Chen Río or Punta Morena). Best all-round pick, not weather-dependent. Jeep hire about USD 60–90 a day, park entries USD 10–20 each (advertised, to be priced).
  • El Cielo sandbar by boat: starfish, rays, waist-deep water. Blue Note will run it on their own boat; good for partners and anyone not diving that afternoon. About USD 50–80 a head (advertised).
  • Town afternoon as the wet-weather fallback: Kaokao chocolate workshop, tequila tasting, the seafront and market.

Nothing at depth the day before two boat days, and an alcohol-light evening.

Add-ons for certified divers (opt-in, priced separately)

  • AOW across Mon–Thu: Blue Note free with eLearning; Sand Dollar USD 380 with eLearning done; Aldora USD 749 all-in.
  • Nitrox: Blue Note course free with eLearning; fills USD 13 a tank at Sand Dollar (about 20 tanks, 6–8 divers set aside), USD 11 at Aldora, MXN 230–260 at Dive Paradise.
  • Night dive Thu: USD 60 shore or 85 boat (Sand Dollar); 74–85 (Aldora); 93 (Dive Paradise).
  • Third dive of the day offered Mon, Tue and Thu afternoons (Dive Paradise 76 one-tank / 135 two-tank, Aldora twilight about 111, Blue Note two-tank afternoon USD 130 plus park fee, advertised; Sand Dollar to be priced). Priced per dive so the many who stop after the morning pay nothing extra.
  • Deep or Drift specialty for a few, to be priced.
  • Bull-shark day at Playa del Carmen (baited, off-island, AOW or 20+ dives, about USD 260): only as a Thursday option for a handful who accept missing group day 1. Not in the core plan.

Options and contingencies

  • Sand Dollar's Friday boat is 13:00, too late for Saturday flyers. Asked for a morning slot. If not: Thu 17 is the last boat day, Fri 18 an early guided shore dive, and the off afternoon moves to Friday, with the island loop as the farewell.
  • Port closure on a training day: Sand Dollar's shore site keeps confined water and the shallow dives going in the lee; Wed closure pushes dive 4 to Thu morning and the new divers join the boats Fri. Two closed days early in the week mean a PADI referral to finish at home.
  • More than 16 students: Sand Dollar runs a second cohort at 13:00; Aldora puts five instructors on 20 at 4:1; Blue Note and Dive Paradise still to answer.
  • Hotel: Casa del Mar (200 yards from Sand Dollar, being priced for us), Hotel Plaza downtown (Blue Note's contracted USD 70–88 a room with breakfast), or Cozumel Hotel & Resort all-inclusive (Dive Paradise, USD 522 a head for six nights, book by 30 Sep).

Where to stay, for an active MBA crowd

Downtown San Miguel

Hotel Plaza (Blue Note's rate USD 70–88 a room, breakfast) or Casa Mexicana

  • Bars, restaurants, seafront and ferry on foot; late nights need no taxis.
  • Blue Note and Aldora minutes away.
  • No beach or house reef. If Sand Dollar is the centre, every student taxis to km 3.2 each morning (USD 8–10 a cab, 10 min).

South hotel zone, km 3–4

Casa del Mar (being priced by Sand Dollar) or Villablanca

  • 200 yards from Sand Dollar's shore site; pool and beach-club access; quiet nights.
  • Taxis or a group shuttle into town for dinner, 10 min.
  • Best if Sand Dollar is the centre: 20 students walk to the 8:30 start.

Cozumel Hotel & Resort, km 1.7, all-inclusive

Dive Paradise's base, USD 522 a head for six nights, book by 30 Sep

  • Everyone under one roof, pool, pier, meals and domestic drinks in; keeps 30–40 together and removes food logistics.
  • 20 min walk or 5 min taxi to town, so nights out still happen.
  • Resort bubble; diving tied to Dive Paradise; rate deadline.

George's week: the leader's dividend

You run the trip on the ground; that stays. These are the transactional upgrades the trip can pay for, disclosed on the sign-up page as the memo already recommends.

The leader pot, by centre (20 certified, 30 people)

  • Blue Note: 10% commission on certified packages (USD 1,200 at 20 divers) plus 1 free package per 5 paid (4 × 540 = 2,160). About USD 3,400 of value, Doug's words: "for the group or the leaders as we choose".
  • Dive Paradise: 10% commission on lodging and diving (about USD 3,300 at 30 people) plus 1 free room-and-diving place per 11 paid (2 places, about 2,200). Largest pot, but the dearest package for everyone else.
  • Aldora: 1 free dive place at 10+ paid. Sand Dollar: 1 free diver. No commission from either.
  • Spend it on: your flights (band USD 570–800, or the Cancún to London leg), a single room, your own dives and gear, then a farewell-dinner kitty for the group.

Your diving, upgraded

  • Sidemount all week on the afternoon dives: rent a rig for the week from Cozumel Dive School (they hold XDeep rigs for their course) and have the group's centre put two AL80s on the boat for you each afternoon. Ask both at booking. Mornings stay backmount while you lead.
  • Private afternoon boat: Blue Note's private two-tank charter is USD 1,000 for up to 10 (advertised), so a leader's boat with 8 of the keen at USD 125 each costs you nothing and picks its own sites. Their private night charter is USD 800.
  • Nitrox on every dive (fills 11–13 a tank), steel 120s if the centre is Aldora, the Devil's Throat day Thursday, night dive Thursday.
  • Comped rental for the leader (ask), or bring only regs and computer.

Your room and your days

  • Single room: at Hotel Plaza's contracted rate a single is USD 70–88 a night, cheaper than most people's share elsewhere; at Dive Paradise a single is USD 840 for the week versus 522 double.
  • Wednesday off afternoon: yours. Sidemount shore session, or the island loop with the group.
  • Friday afternoon: last dive by 13:00, then a beach club (Money Bar or Buccanos) rather than logistics; the farewell dinner is pre-booked by the social chair.
  • Saturday: Cancún to London direct, no airport shepherding.

Sidemount for George

  • Within Blue Note or Sand Dollar: no rig. Both can put a second tank on the boat; neither lists sidemount harness or regulator rental.
  • Rig rental exists on the island. Cozumel Dive School holds XDeep rigs (with computer and DSMB) for its PADI Sidemount course; as a certified sidemount diver the ask at booking time is a week's rig rental without the course, then two AL80s per afternoon dive from the group's centre. Nobody has been asked.
  • Aldora accommodates sidemount divers on its boats and Jungle Divers rents pro-valve tanks, but both assume you bring the rig.
