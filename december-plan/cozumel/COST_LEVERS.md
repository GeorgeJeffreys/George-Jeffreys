# Cozumel: driving the fee down at scale (research note, 24 Sep 2026)

George's brief: keep the quality, use the group's scale (20–40 divers), find a cheaper way to insure everyone, cut the fee. Web fetch is blocked, so everything here is from search snippets (ADVERTISED) unless marked QUOTED. Nothing below has been asked of Blue Note yet; the questions are queued in ROUTINE.md and go to Doug in the next natural reply, not as separate pings.

## Fee today (rebuilt 24 Sep): USD 1,400 beginner / 1,300 certified
Certified: package 600, rental 150, park 65, hotel 246, leader share 71, contingency 68, rounded up from 1,200. Beginner: course and two boat days 640, rental 60, park 39, eLearning 230, hotel 246, leader share 71, contingency 77, rounded up from 1,363. DAN and the tax buffer are already out.

## 1. Dive-accident cover: cheaper than USD 119 a head
| option | per head | how | flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| DAN short-term dive accident coverage, 6–10 days | USD 50 (3–5 days USD 40) | sold only through a DAN Business Member dive operator, bought on arrival or before; underwritten for US residents (National Union Fire Insurance); USD 200,000 medical, evacuation, USD 10,000 death benefit | ADVERTISED (world.dan.org, apps.dan.org). | DAN annual: membership USD 40 + Master plan USD 44 | USD 84 | each person buys online; USD 125,000 dive-accident limit; covers Thanksgiving and Egypt too for anyone doing more than one trip | ADVERTISED (dan.org comparison page). The 119 we assumed was membership + Preferred (USD 79) |
| DiveAssure DiveSafe 1-week plan | price not in snippets; annual about EUR 70, so a week is likely USD 30–50 | bought online per person | QUOTE NEEDED (diveassure.com) |

Recommendation: the short-term DAN plan at USD 50 through the shop is the cheapest reliable cover if Blue Note sells it; otherwise DAN Master at USD 84. Either way it stays an own cost on the sign-up page, as George decided, with the price shown. A "group DAN" discount does not exist in the snippets: DAN's sponsor programme is for shops, not per-trip groups.

## 2. Gear rental: ask for a group or weekly rate
Blue Note quoted USD 30/day (QUOTED). Its own site shows USD 25 per diver on private charters; Salty Endeavors USD 20/day, Sand Dollar 25, Jungle Divers 25, Blue Magic 24 with multi-day discounts (all ADVERTISED). With 20–40 divers all renting five days, USD 20–25/day is a fair ask: saves USD 25–50 a head on the certified fee. ASK DOUG (batched).

## 3. Marine park fee
Blue Note's site: USD 12 / 220 pesos a day; the park raised it to 225 pesos (about USD 13) in 2026 (ADVERTISED, Blue Note price page and cozumeldivehub). Budget moved from 15 to 13 a day. Fixed by the government; no group lever.

## 4. Open Water eLearning
PADI's store price is about USD 230 (ADVERTISED, store.padi.com); the budget had 195 and now carries 230. Levers: Blue Note can usually issue eLearning codes at its own price (ASK DOUG, batched); PADI's referral offer is 15% off Open Water eLearning (ADVERTISED, padi.com/promotions); Black Friday sales run 20–25% off but 27 Nov is late for a 13 Dec course. Saves up to USD 35–45 a student.

## 5. Comps and package price at scale
Blue Note gives one free certified package per five paid (QUOTED) and nothing on Open Water. At 40 people the group earns about three comps; one is George's, the spare two are already credited against the invoice in the Group sheet. ASK DOUG (batched): a free Open Water place per eight or ten students, or a lower package or course price once the group passes 30. Saving if granted: USD 800–1,200 across the group, about USD 20–40 a head.

## 6. Lodging: Hotel Plaza versus the Casa Mexicana sister hotels
Hotel Plaza (50 rooms, pool, downtown, breakfast) at USD 70/88 a room via Blue Note (QUOTED) is USD 246 a head for six nights. Booking sites show it from about USD 64 a night before tax (ADVERTISED), so the contracted rate is fair, not cheap. Alternative in the same streets: Suites Colonial (28 units, breakfast at Casa Mexicana, pool at the sister hotel) is advertised around USD 50 a night for a two-room suite with kitchenette (ADVERTISED, older snippet), and Suites Bahia opposite the dive-boat pier. At USD 50 a room the hotel line falls to about USD 150 a head, saving about USD 100. Quality is a step down (no pool on site, older rooms) but still walk-to-everything. Casa Mexicana has been emailed and is silent; its 25 Sep chaser asks for group tiers of 10, 15 and 20 rooms across Casa Mexicana, Suites Bahia and Suites Colonial. George chooses once both quotes are in.

## 7. Things not worth touching
Contingency (6%, refunded if unused); rounding (posting 1,350 / 1,250 saves 50 on paper only); the beginners' Thursday and Friday boat days (USD 330 off the beginner fee but it removes the two real reef mornings that sell the course); third dives and the island loop are already outside the fee.

## Where the fee could land
If Doug agrees a USD 20–25 rental rate and the eLearning code at cost, and the group takes the sister hotels at about USD 50 a room: certified about USD 1,100, beginner about USD 1,200, with dive cover at USD 50 on top as an own cost. With Hotel Plaza kept: about 1,200 / 1,300.
