Cozumel Dive Week
Sun 13 – Sat 19 Dec 2026

Six nights on the island with the best drift diving in the Caribbean. Get certified from scratch in three days, or dive the Palancar walls every morning. Led by George Jeffreys (former PADI Master Scuba Diver Trainer), diving with Blue Note Scuba, staying at Hotel Plaza in downtown San Miguel.

How to sign up

  1. Reply in the group chat with your name, track (Beginner or Certified) and whether you want a room-mate assigned.
  2. Pay the $300 deposit on CampusGroups by Sat 10 Oct. Balance due Fri 6 Nov.
  3. Beginners: buy PADI Open Water eLearning through the link we send and finish it before you fly. Everyone: DAN insurance and a dive medical form.
  4. Book your own flights to Cozumel (CZM) once your place is confirmed. Arrive Sunday, leave Saturday.

Deposits refundable in full until 6 Nov, then only if your place is resold. George's place is comped by the dive shop and the shop's group commission goes to him for organising, as is standard for group leaders.

Prices from Blue Note Scuba's and Hotel Plaza's quotes of 23 Sep 2026 and will be locked when we book. Flights and food are estimates. Capacity is 40; places go in order of deposit.
