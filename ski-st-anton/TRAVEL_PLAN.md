## Ski kit
- **Lift pass:** 6-day Ski Arlberg pass, Sun 10 – Fri 15, €468 (peak season, provisional). Buy online in advance and collect at the ticket machines.
- **Rental:** Sport Alber. Chesa guests get free ski and boot storage there. Book online for the online discount: about €150–250 for 6 days, plus a helmet.
- **Insurance:** winter sports cover that includes mountain rescue.
- **Money:**
  - Chesa takes **no cards on site**.
  - Balance €3,275 + tourist tax. Pay it by bank transfer before arrival, or bring cash.
