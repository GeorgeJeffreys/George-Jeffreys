Where we could stay in St Anton

Five places that are free for our week and keep the whole group in one building. Prices are per person for all 7 nights, sharing, including the €5 a night tourist tax.

St Anton The village

Main lifts (Galzig, Rendl), the pedestrian centre and the après bars. Chesa Platina is on the quieter west edge, so it is a bus ride from the centre.

Nasserein The east end of St Anton

Its own gondola into the same ski area, and you can ski back down to it. The centre is a 10–15 min walk or a few minutes on the free ski bus.
