# Round 6 brief: hotels with rooms free for 9–16 Jan (8 Oct 2026)

Read `RULES.md` in this folder first. Its web-access and read-only rules still apply: no enquiry forms, emails or bookings.

- **Group:** 10 people. Couples share doubles; the rest are happy in twins, triples and family rooms.
- **Dates:** Sat 9 – Sat 16 Jan 2027, 7 nights. Note any hotel that only takes Sunday arrivals or minimum stays.
- **What counts:** hotels, hotel-garnis (B&B hotels), guest houses and pensions with rooms, not apartments. **All 10 must be in the same hotel.** Find the cheapest combination of free rooms that sleeps exactly 10, e.g. 5 doubles/twins, or 2 triples + 2 doubles.
- **Meals:** say whether the price is room only, B&B or half board. B&B is the normal target. Half board is fine to report, but flag it.
- **Price:** the date-specific total for 7 nights, then € per person per night **including** the €5 tourist tax: (total + 350) ÷ 70. Report everything up to about €150 per person per night, cheapest first, and flag anything under €90.
- **Also record:**
  - walk in minutes to the nearest lift, and which lift;
  - stars or category;
  - sauna or spa if the hotel has one;
  - whether rooms share bathrooms;
  - cancellation terms if shown.
- **Sources:**
  - the tourist office's Deskline engine, filtered to hotels, garnis and pensions;
  - the hotels' own booking engines;
  - Booking.com, if you can get a date-specific price; otherwise give the link.

Add the columns `stars`, `room_mix_for_10`, `board`, `pp_per_night_incl_tax_eur`, `walk_min_to_lift`, `sauna_spa` and `shared_bathrooms` after the RULES columns.

Final reply under 200 words: the best five for your area, with the € per person per night including tax, board, walk time and room mix, plus anything to check by hand.
