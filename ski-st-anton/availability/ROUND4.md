# Round 4 brief: one self-catering place for the whole group, closer in (6 Oct 2026)

Read `RULES.md` in this folder first. Dates, statuses, web-access rules and the read-only rule still apply: no enquiry forms, emails or bookings. This brief narrows the target.

## What the organiser wants

- **One roof.** The whole group (12, ideally stretching to 13–15) under one roof, self-catering. Best is one apartment or chalet. Acceptable is a whole building of flats where we take every flat, or flats in the **same building**, like Chesa Platina. **Never** separate houses, even next door.
- **Closer to the lifts than the current pick.** The benchmark is Chesa Platina (Stadleweg 26, Stadle): 3 flats in one house, €5,713 for 7 nights, **€68 per person per night at 12**, about 1.4 km (~20 min walk) to the Galzigbahn. Anything we propose should be:
  - within about 10 minutes' walk of the Galzigbahn, Nassereinbahn or Rendlbahn;
  - or closer than Chesa Platina at a similar price.
- **Budget is the constraint.** Report everything up to about **€90 per person per night at 12**. Flag anything under €75.
- **Areas:** St Anton (Dorf/centre, Oberdorf, Gastig, Moos, Mooserweg, Stadle, Rosanna area) and Nasserein only.
- **Beds:** real beds are preferred. A sofa bed or two is acceptable; count them, and say so.

## How to report

For each candidate, add a column `beds_vs_sofabeds`, e.g. "10 beds + 2 sofa", plus a walking time in minutes to the nearest lift and which lift. Rank by:
1. one unit first, then a whole building;
2. walking time;
3. price.

Final reply under 200 words: the best three or four, each with the total for 7 nights, € per person per night at 12 (and at 15 if possible), walk time, layout and link, and what the organiser should check by hand.
