## 5. Booking mechanics for a group house

- **Saturday-to-Saturday is the norm in January.** Chalet Gabl in St Anton, for example, lists only 7-night, Saturday check-in slots for January 2027 (2–9 Jan, 9–16 Jan, 16–23 Jan, 23–30 Jan) (ADVERTISED snippet, https://www.allchalets.com/holiday-rentals/28885-chalet-in-st-anton-austria). UK operators (SNO, Inghams, Iglu) sell catered chalets as Saturday 7-night packages, e.g. "departing 2 January 2027" (ADVERTISED snippet, https://www.sno.co.uk/ski-chalets/st-anton-chalets/). "Short stays" offered by luxury operators appear to be for low-season periods only (ADVERTISED snippet, brambleski.com).
- **Implication for Mon 4 to Sat 9 Jan (dates fixed; INFERRED):** a Monday arrival at a whole chalet or house will often be refused or charged as the full 2–9 Jan week, which is the pricier Christmas/New Year band. That means paying 7 nights for 5. Options: (a) ask owners directly whether they will sell the stay and leave a 2-night gap (Sat 2 to Mon 4); some will in the post-New-Year lull. (b) Look at hotels, pensions, hostels and apartment houses, which are usually more flexible about arrival day (PRIOR KNOWLEDGE). (c) Budget for paying 7 nights. When comparing options, the effective per-night price is the weekly price ÷ 5. QUOTE NEEDED from each property on the minimum stay for these dates.
- **Tourist tax (Aufenthaltsabgabe / Ortstaxe), St Anton:** **EUR 5.00 per person per night** (USD 5.85), the Tyrolean maximum, in force since about Nov 2023. It applies from age 15 (or 16, depending on the establishment) (ADVERTISED snippet, https://tirol.orf.at/stories/3208324/, https://www.meinbezirk.at/landeck/c-lokales/tvb-st-anton-am-arlberg-erhoeht-ortstaxe-auf-fuenf-euro_a6135764). **12 people × 5 nights = EUR 300 total (EUR 25 pp)** (INFERRED). It is usually added at the property, on top of the quoted rate.
- **Guest registration:** Austrian hosts must register every guest (Meldepflicht), now mostly electronically, so each guest needs passport details at check-in (PRIOR KNOWLEDGE, consistent with the ecoach.at snippet on electronic guest registration).
- **Short-term rental rules:**
  - Tirol has tightened rules for tourist rentals. Every online-listed rental must be registered with the local tourism association and given a registration number (ADVERTISED snippet, https://tirol.orf.at/stories/3372171/, https://www.tt.com/artikel/30942124/).
  - **EU Regulation 2024/1028 applies from 20 May 2026.** Platforms (Airbnb, Booking.com) may no longer show a short-term rental without a valid registration number (ADVERTISED snippet, https://calvest.de/neue-airbnb-gesetze-2026-was-vermieter-jetzt-wissen-muessen/).
  - Tirol also caps vacation residences (Freizeitwohnsitze) by municipality (ADVERTISED snippet, law-experts.at).
  - "Privatzimmervermietung" (private room letting) is limited to **max 3 units / 10 beds**, with the owner living in the building (ADVERTISED snippet, https://tirol.orf.at/stories/3264869/). A legitimate private-room landlord therefore cannot sleep 12 under one roof on that licence. A 12+ bed house will be a commercially licensed apartment house, chalet or Pension (INFERRED).
  - **Practical rule:** only book listings that display a registration number. An unregistered whole-house Airbnb risks cancellation (INFERRED).
- **Deposits:** Austrian chalets and apartment houses typically take a 30–50% deposit at booking, with the balance 4–8 weeks before arrival, and cancellation terms tighten inside about 6 weeks (PRIOR KNOWLEDGE; QUOTE NEEDED per property). Collect the group's deposits before committing.
